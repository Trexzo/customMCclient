## Concrete production source problem
`Minecraft189JumpResetModule.apply` correctly detects a **fresh genuine local player hurt-time rising edge** from an actual player snapshot, enforces ground/forward/sneak/sprint/current GUI/chance/cooldown and consumes the hurt edge *before* calling original mapped `EntityPlayer.customMcJump()`. A reentrancy guard prevents a second nested native jump, including after the real jump throws. However, when the genuine native jump synchronously disables→re-enables JumpReset, or edits persisted GUI cooldown/requireForward/pauseSneak/chance/requireSprint, the old callback still returned `true` and left a pending synthetic cooldown owned by the old policy. An original jump can physically mutate the player before callback returns; we must not pretend that mutation was undone or that the new activation authorized it. This source/lifetime bug is independent of V26 scaffold world ray.

Do not forge rollback of the vanilla jump that has already physically happened.

If policy changed during that throwing native callback in the same activation, revoke only its stale synthetic cooldown and rethrow the actual original failure; never manufacture success. Preserve original zero-cooldown/one-jump-per-real-hurt semantics, movement and GUI focus gates, bounded 0–100 chance, native sprint and grounded requirements.
- No fake incoming damage packet, no invented hurtTime, player position, original motion, synthetic jump or outgoing controller action.

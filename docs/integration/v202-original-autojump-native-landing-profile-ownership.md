# V202 — actual airborne-to-grounded AutoJump contact owns its persisted eligibility policy

## Why this is an actual source behavior issue
`Minecraft189AutoJumpModule` schedules a real mapped `customMcJump()` from the source player's available original ground, sneak and sprint state plus physically held forward and WASD keys. An observed original air-to-ground transition owns a bounded countdown and a single native jump. Prior work pinned **Landing Delay** at initial contact and disarmed the contact if the delay was edited, even after its countdown expired. However other persisted policies — **Require Forward**, **Require Movement**, **Require Sprinting**, and **Pause While Sneaking** — were not owned by the same contact. For example, a real on-ground source with `Require Forward=true` and no W remains pending; toggling Require Forward OFF via ClickGUI could later consume that stale grounded contact and perform a jump without a new source landing. This is a direct state-machine observation; no native Mojang crash is claimed.

## V202 production change
- When an actually observed original airborne→grounded transition captures its current Landing Delay, capture all four currently persisted movement admission switches at **the same genuine contact**.
- Before proceeding with each pending grounded callback or delayed eligibility, require *all* of Landing Delay, Require Forward, Require Movement, Require Sprinting and Pause While Sneaking still equal the captured policy. If any changed since that same contact, **disarm it** and require another observed original airborne→grounded transition before issuing `customMcJump()`.
- Preserve the exact existing behavior where genuine **physical key or native sprint state** becomes eligible during the *same unchanged policy* contact. A W key press or actual original sprint bit transition is NOT treated as a GUI policy edit. Preserve original source preconditions, ClickGUI disarming, sprint/sneak state, bounded delay, consume-before-original-jump exception replay protection, and initial onEnable grounded-jump compatibility. No fake airborne state, player jump counter, packets or bypassing native motion.

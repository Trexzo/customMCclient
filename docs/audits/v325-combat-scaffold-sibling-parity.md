## Scope and method

Preserved nonblank, noncomment donor-added code lines are useful evidence but **not** an equivalence proof: changed source may be reorganized, enhanced, or deliberately more restrictive. Historical Git divergence alone cannot justify merging.

## Interpretation and safety boundaries

- **No automatic cherry-picks** are justified by this source comparison: the identified safety and real-game behavior is present, extended, or deliberately stricter in V325. Some historical diagnostic-only markers, slowness fixtures, standalone tests and helper APIs are absent; **do not claim exact equivalence where not shown**.
- #526 novel UUID evidence **must not** weaken V325's second mandatory net +4 gate; the two original witnesses are intentionally stricter together. The existing `Minecraft189WorldEntityUuidState` globally rejects duplicate IDs before exposing snapshots; V325 `m273NovelNativeNonplayerCount` is not the sole validator.
- No diagnostic, helper/parser or mocked position proves a real controller click, original World observation or vanilla server damage. Only authentic pinned original binary CI and server logs support the concrete game acceptance claims.
- V325 remains separately **2 × 80/80 original Minecraft 1.8.9 PASS on unchanged exact head**, and both OS Foundation success, but historical intermittent V319 early B=10 remains unresolved root cause; do not weaken B=0, UUID/ray/FOV/Wall/Scaffold/0.12/0.30/20-sample checks.
- This audit is limited to the **ten specified sibling PRs**, not the entire universe of 37 diverged heads. Exact historical test parity and rights for redistributing binaries remain outside this audit. Retain Draft status and release security/license/Windows-package blockers.
